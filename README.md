
# My Tech Companion 💙

### Friendly, Patient & Personalized Tech Assistance

My Tech Companion provides approachable, in-home technology assistance for individuals and families, with a special focus on helping seniors feel more comfortable and confident using technology.

## Our Services

- Smartphone & Tablet Assistance
- Computer & Laptop Help
- Wi-Fi & Internet Troubleshooting
- Smart TV & Streaming Setup
- Email, Apps & Online Accounts
- Printers & Device Setup
- One-on-One Technology Lessons
- General Tech Help

## Our Mission

Technology should make life easier, not harder.

Our goal is to provide friendly, patient, judgment-free assistance so everyone can feel more confident using everyday technology.
